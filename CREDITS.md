# Profile artwork

The animated header is rendered from [Thomas Chuang’s interactive portfolio](https://thomas-chuang.com/#proj-historical-map-geoai). The name remains fixed while modern satellite imagery rewinds to the same location on a 1921 map, which is then outlined, extracted, scanned, classified, and turned into a 3D landscape.

- Satellite imagery: [Esri World Imagery](https://services.arcgisonline.com/ArcGIS/rest/services/World_Imagery/MapServer), © Esri and Vantor. The visible crop contains imagery acquired in 2021–2025, so the animation labels the mosaic “2020s,” rather than claiming one capture year. Imagery and historical maps use matching Web Mercator extents; the release includes source metadata and the crop registration.
- Historical map: 1921 Taiwan map, Academia Sinica [Taiwan Historical Maps](https://gissrv4.sinica.edu.tw/gis/twhgis/), layer `JM20K_1921`.
- Classification: Thomas Chuang’s recorded sliding-window model output. The network activity is illustrative; percentages show each class’s share of the classified pixels in the complete map, excluding unclassified pixels. They are area percentages, not confidence scores.
- Elevation: Ministry of the Interior [20 m digital terrain model](https://data.gov.tw/dataset/176927), displayed with 6× vertical exaggeration.
- Houses and vegetation are cartographic symbols placed within predicted classes, not recovered building footprints or measured counts.
- Technology marks: [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), distributed through react-icons. Names and logos belong to their respective owners.
- University emblems: the NCCU, Peking University, and UC Berkeley marks used in the portfolio’s education section. The original colors are retained; labels distinguish undergraduate study from exchanges. The emblems belong to their respective universities.

Animation files and static posters are hosted as [versioned release assets](https://github.com/chuang091/chuang091/releases/tag/profile-art-v9), outside the Git history. Each card selects one image for the viewer’s screen and color scheme, and a static poster when reduced motion is requested.

The complete 24-second loop retains the satellite comparison, map extraction, neural-network illustration, classification, and 3D terrain growth. The name and palette sit above the animation, giving the artwork the full card width. The classification readout has more space between the map, heading, and rows. Desktop animation delivery is 1920 × 1824; mobile is 1152 × 1303. Full-color animated AVIF is preferred, with an animated WebP fallback at 1600 × 1520 (desktop) or 1152 × 1303 (mobile), and a static poster for reduced motion. Only the selected format, layout, and theme are requested. Exact sizes, encoding settings, and SHA-256 checksums are recorded in the release manifest.

## Map projects

Two clickable animations below the contact links use the same recorded geography as the interactive portfolio.

- **Convenience-store analysis:** 1,534 recorded store positions from the [original feature service](https://services6.arcgis.com/gDvgvk6tNkAnOdO4/arcgis/rest/services/%E8%B6%85%E5%95%861082nd/FeatureServer/0), registered to the Taipei analysis bounds in EPSG:3826. The city unfolds into road connections, population, bus stops, MRT stations, and YouBike stations; the original vectors become display-normalized factor grids and combine into the [recorded suitability result](https://github.com/chuang091/MGWR/tree/main/Results/Suitability). These are research data, not a live inventory or proposed store locations.
- **City outline:** the alpha contour of the [Taipei City Government zoning dataset](https://data.taipei/dataset/detail?id=3bab0a01-7936-4218-8cb5-f74dfcb43dda), used as a neutral silhouette behind the stores.
- **YouBike flow:** 658 paths derived from the [original vector-field raster](https://tiledimageservices6.arcgis.com/gDvgvk6tNkAnOdO4/arcgis/rest/services/c10000/ImageServer). Moving trails illustrate that field, rather than individual recorded trips or measured cycling speed. The full research project is [YouBikeAnalysis](https://github.com/chuang091/YouBikeAnalysis).
- **Weekday and weekend journeys:** the top 64 routes by aggregate frequency in each mode, from the [original weekday/weekend OD files](https://github.com/tzu901/youbikeViz/tree/3f61f8bc3222e7f609c391215cd8b6359999b2d9). Both endpoints are within the downtown study area. Curves connect recorded station endpoints; they are not street trajectories. The source excludes frequencies below 500, and day counts are unknown, so these are not per-day rates.
- **Temporal clusters:** the original cluster IDs, positions, and timestamps of 1,317 records in the [DBSCAN feature layer](https://services6.arcgis.com/gDvgvk6tNkAnOdO4/arcgis/rest/services/YoubikeDBSCAN/FeatureServer/5), spanning 15 January 2020 to 29 September 2023. Height represents time, color identifies the recorded cluster, and gray denotes noise.
- **Transit context:** the [study's MRT layer](https://services6.arcgis.com/gDvgvk6tNkAnOdO4/arcgis/rest/services/%E5%8C%97%E5%8C%97%E6%8D%B7%E9%81%8B%E8%B7%AF%E7%B7%9A/FeatureServer/11) provides geographic context; it is not a date-specific reconstruction of the MRT network.
- **Contemporary street context:** © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), ODbL. Analysis colors, vertical layer separation, and camera movement are display choices.

The map cards use 1920 × 1440 desktop animations and 1152 × 1980 portrait animations, with separate light and dark palettes. Animated AVIF is preferred; WebP and static reduced-motion posters are included. Factor grids, the assembled layers, and the final result hold before the next transformation. The header's neural illustration and classification retain their longer reading time. Source hashes and frame durations are included in the release manifest and map-source metadata.

The [artwork release](https://github.com/chuang091/chuang091/releases/tag/profile-art-v9) presents YouBike first, followed by convenience-store analysis. YouBike starts with downtown flow, pans within that view, and compares weekday and weekend station-to-station journeys in the same framing, with 2.5 seconds to read each result. It then turns into a dated DBSCAN reveal through 2020–2023 and holds the completed cloud before returning to the city. Its loop lasts 27 seconds; the convenience-store loop lasts 15.7 seconds. WebP fallbacks are 1600 × 1200 on desktop or 960 × 1650 on mobile.

The animation release preserves every frame and its duration. AVIF keyframes are no more than 15 frames apart, and each frame has 36% fewer pixels to decode. Animated WebP fallbacks and reduced-motion posters remain on [the previous release](https://github.com/chuang091/chuang091/releases/tag/profile-art-v8). The release manifest records source hashes, dimensions, frame counts, and keyframe positions; `performance.json` reports playback measurements and download sizes.
